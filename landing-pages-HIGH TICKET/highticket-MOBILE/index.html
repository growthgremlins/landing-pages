OUR SERVICES

Everything your vehicle needs — done at your door.
Proudly serving Lakeland & all of Polk County, FL.

Exterior Wash & Decontamination

Multi-stage hand wash lifts road film, brake dust and bug residue, then clay bar and iron remover pull out the bonded contamination a wash leaves behind. Wheels, tires and door jambs included — paint that is genuinely clean, not just rinsed.

Paint Correction & Polishing

Machine polishing cuts away swirl marks, wash scratches and oxidation to bring the gloss back. It is the step that restores depth and reflection to tired paint — the difference you can see from across the driveway.

Ceramic Coating & Protection

Long-term ceramic protection applied over decontaminated, polished paint — up to a 5-year coating. Water beads and rolls off, dirt struggles to stick, and every wash after gets dramatically easier. Ceramic sealant is also built into our Luxury Detail.

Headlight Restoration

Cloudy, yellowed lenses are wet-sanded, polished and UV-sealed back to clarity. Brighter output for Florida night driving and an instantly newer-looking front end.

Deep Interior Cleaning & Detailing

A full interior reset: deep vacuum, steam cleaning, and an air-compressor blowout that drives dust and grit out of vents, seat rails and every crack and crevice. Dash, console, cup holders, door panels and glass all finished by hand.

Leather Care & Conditioning

Leather is cleaned, then conditioned to put moisture back into the hide so it stays soft and stops cracking. Added UV protection keeps your seats from fading and hardening in the Florida sun.

Carpet & Upholstery Shampooing

Carpet and seat shampoo with hot-water extraction pulls stains, spills and ground-in dirt up and out instead of pushing them around. Pet hair, kid messes and years of daily driving — handled.

Odor Removal & Sanitization

Smoke, pet, mildew and food odors are treated at the source with steam and professional sanitizers rather than masked with fragrance. Your cabin comes back neutral, fresh and genuinely clean.
