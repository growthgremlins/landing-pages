OUR SERVICES

Everything your vehicle needs — done at your door.
Proudly serving Melbourne & all of Brevard County, FL.

Exterior Wash & Decontamination

Multi-stage hand wash removes all dirt, grime, and bonded contaminants. Clay bar treatment ensures your paint is clean down to the surface—ready for protection or just looking its best.

Paint Correction & Polishing

Machine polishing removes swirl marks, scratches, and oxidation to restore your paint’s original gloss. Brings back depth and clarity that washing alone can’t achieve.

Ceramic Coating & Protection

Nano-ceramic coating shields your paint from water, dirt, and UV damage — 6-month up to 10-year protection available. Hydrophobic protection makes maintenance effortless and keeps your car looking freshly detailed.

Headlight Restoration

Restore cloudy, yellowed headlights to crystal clarity through sanding, polishing, and UV sealing. Safer night driving and a better-looking vehicle in one service.

Deep Interior Cleaning & Detailing

Complete interior refresh with vacuum, steam cleaning, and every surface detailed. Dashboard, console, cup holders, vents, windows, and door panels—all spotless.

Leather Care & Conditioning

Professional leather cleaning and conditioning restores moisture, prevents cracking, and enhances color. UV protection keeps your seats supple and rich-looking year after year.

Carpet & Upholstery Shampooing

Hot water extraction lifts deep stains, odors, and allergens from carpets and seats. Pet stains, food spills, and ground-in dirt removed at the source.

Odor Removal & Sanitization

Eliminate smoke, pet, mildew, and food odors with ozone treatment and professional sanitizers. Kills bacteria at the source and leaves your cabin smelling fresh.

Melbourne Mobile Detailing FAQ

Everything you need to know before booking.

Mobile detailing means we bring everything to you — water, power, equipment, and products. You don't drive anywhere or sit in a waiting room. We show up at your home, apartment, or workplace in Melbourne, FL and detail your car right in your driveway or parking spot. The result is the same showroom-quality finish as a shop, without any of the inconvenience.
📍 We serve Melbourne & Brevard County, FL
It depends on the package and your vehicle's condition. Our Standard Detail typically takes around 2 hours. The Premium Detail runs about 3 hours for a thorough inside-and-out deep clean. The Elite Detail takes 4–5 hours for our most comprehensive treatment. We'll give you a time estimate when you call so you can plan your day.
We use only premium, professional-grade detailing products that are safe for all surfaces — including pH-balanced shampoos, paint-safe clay bars, UV-protective conditioners, and ceramic-infused sealants. Every product is carefully selected to protect your vehicle's finish while delivering showroom-quality results.
A car wash cleans the surface. Mobile detailing restores and protects the entire vehicle. We hand-wash every panel, decontaminate the paint, clean inside every vent and crevice, condition leather, shampoo carpet, protect surfaces with sealants, and more. Think of it as a deep spa treatment for your car versus a quick rinse. The results last weeks to months, not hours.
100%. If you're not satisfied with any part of your detail, tell us before we leave and we'll fix it on the spot — no questions asked. We don't consider a job done until you're happy with every panel. That's the TMCleansedrive standard.
